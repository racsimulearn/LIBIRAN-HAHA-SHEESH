RAC SimuLEARN

Interactive Refrigeration Cycle Trainer

An interactive learning platform that helps students visualize refrigeration-cycle operation, components, pressure conditions, and electrical controls, alongside the physical trainer.

Early version: the refrigerant path follows the standard vapor-compression cycle and is awaiting confirmation against the physical trainer. Items marked “to be verified” are not yet confirmed.
